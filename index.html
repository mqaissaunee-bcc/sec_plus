Maximum 90 questions (multiple-choice and performance-based), 90 minutes duration, 750/900 passing score. Current exam version with latest cybersecurity trends.

Comprehensive study guides, interactive content, real-world scenarios, practice questions, and hands-on lab exercises for each domain.
