[NO BUFFER] Fifteen weeks with three exam weeks leaves no review week and no slack. A campus closure or holiday falling on a class day costs a module week directly. Module 10 is the densest in the course and is the first candidate to split across two sessions or move partly to asynchronous Canvas work.
[CALENDAR COLLISIONS] Three teaching weeks contain a US federal holiday, and one is serious. Week 15 (21 December) holds Unit Exam 3 in the week of Christmas — most academic calendars end the term before this, so the exam almost certainly needs to move earlier or the term needs to start before 14 September. Week 11 (23 November) contains Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November and carries Module 9, one of the heavier modules. Week 9 (9 November) contains Veterans Day on Wednesday 11 November. Week 13 has no lab and is the only slack in the schedule if a week has to be recovered.

No exam is cumulative. Unit boundaries do not align to domain boundaries — domain sizes make that impossible — so each exam is defined by its module range. Students should study by module rather than by domain; review material organized by domain gives the wrong scope for Exam 2.

Seventeen are scheduled, at no more than two in any week, and each sits in the week whose module supplies the underlying concepts so the reading precedes the lab. The remaining eight are not assigned. Six duplicate material another scheduled lab already covers; two were withdrawn from this term. [LAB LOAD] No week carries more than two labs. Weeks 3, 4, 7 and 14 carry one, and Week 13 (Module 11, Governance and Risk Management) carries none — no lab in the catalogue addresses policy, risk analysis, or business impact. Module 11 has three built-in analysis labs covering that ground, including the SLE, ARO, and ALE calculations, so Week 13 is the natural place to absorb a lost class day or to schedule exam review.
[IF CAPACITY ALLOWS] Weeks 4 and 7 now have an open second slot. The closest fits from the unscheduled list are Lab 11 (RADIUS) for Week 7, since 802.1X and EAP are taught in Module 6 and otherwise get no hands-on time, and Lab 05 for Week 4. Lab 09 (Group Policy) remains the other strong candidate wherever a slot opens.

Module structure

Each module is a self-contained interactive reading in the online textbook. Modules carry the teaching content and an ungraded self-check assessment with unlimited retries and an 80% target. Graded exams, lab assignments, and due dates live in Canvas.
